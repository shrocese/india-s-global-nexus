# India's Global Nexus

I'm thinking about creating foreign policy related website and the interface can be innovative content. I am thinking of a structured content and at the heart of it would be bilateral relations. now, if I do bilateral for every country, then the permeation combination is a lot but also we have Wikipedia as a source supplier, but I don't just want a simple Vicky consolidation.. I wanted more optimised, more concrete, more precise and factual correct, and then also enable current affairs. Let's keep one country at centre that will be India and then try to build an interface that shows all of India's bilateral relations and any user can click on the whatever to see India's bilateral relations with other countries. The content then been include basic comparisons such as territory, population, GDP, per capita income, but the relations part is the main thing which will involve history of relations, pre-independence and then post independence of India, and then all the sectors of cooperation or conflict from political, geographical, strategic,  technology, scientific, society that is people to people, business, Diaspora, agreements, recent development and so on.. so how do we go about it?
